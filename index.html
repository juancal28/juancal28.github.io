01

Quant News-RAG Trading System

An autonomous quantitative trading system that ingests financial news via RSS feeds, builds a semantic knowledge base in Qdrant, and scores sentiment using FinBERT. A RAG agent queries recent news to propose citation-backed strategy adjustments, which are validated against risk limits and backtested on 1+ year of historical data before requiring human approval. Performance-critical execution and backtesting logic is compiled to native C++ via pybind11. Paper trades $100K simulated capital with a 2% daily-loss circuit breaker.

01

Microorganisms

2023  ·  Cited 4 times

Published

“In Silico Identification and In Vitro Validation of Repurposed Compounds Targeting the RSV Polymerase”

Eric Xu, Seohyun Park, Juan Calderon, Dongdong Cao, Bo Liang

Respiratory Syncytial Virus (RSV) is the top cause of infant hospitalization globally, with no effective treatments available. This study used in silico computational analysis including molecular docking and protein-ligand simulation of a database containing 6,554 molecules to identify top repurposed compound candidates against the RSV polymerase, including Micafungin, Totrombopag, and Verubecestat. We validated Micafungin's inhibition of the RSV RdRP using an in vitro transcription assay, showing significant improvements over current inhibitors.

03

Georgia Junior Science & Humanities Symposium

2024

Presented

“Rethinking Carbon Dioxide Reduction through Heterogeneous Catalyst Precursor Molecule Development”

Juan Calderon

I developed novel copper-based catalyst precursors for efficient carbon dioxide reduction, addressing challenges in energy efficiency, product selectivity, and scalability. My work focused on synthesizing and characterizing diimine and N-heterocyclic carbene copper complexes, utilizing advanced organometallic techniques (inert atmosphere, NMR spectroscopy, vacuum filtration). These precursors showed promise for selective carbon–carbon bond formation in CO₂ reduction, supporting efforts to convert atmospheric CO₂ into valuable hydrocarbons and advance sustainable energy solutions.
